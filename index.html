Review: CyberSecure Router X100

Published by Alex Chen | Rating: 4.5/5 Stars

The CyberSecure Router X100 is the latest flagship networking hub designed for smart homes. In our latency benchmarks, it clocked an impressive 2ms ping under heavy load, outperforming its predecessor by 35%.

Security features include hardware-isolated guest networks, automated firmware patching, and WPA3 encryption standard out of the box. Retail pricing starts at $189.99, making it a highly competitive choice for power users.
